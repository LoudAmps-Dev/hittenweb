Bio

HITTEN is Spain's most relentless rock 'n roll export — a five-piece heavy metal machine forged in Murcia in 2011 and built to last. Equal parts arena swagger and street-level grit, the band channels the golden era of hard rock and classic heavy metal into something that feels viscerally alive: screaming twin guitars, anthemic hooks, and a rhythm section that hits like a freight train.

In over a decade of non-stop activity, HITTEN has toured the US, sold out two consecutive nights in Tokyo, and stormed stages across Europe — earning headlining slots and coveted festival spots at Wacken Open Air, Rock Imperium, and beyond. They are not a nostalgia act. They are the thing itself.

Signed to Germany's legendary High Roller Records, the band has released five studio albums — most recently While Passion Lasts (2023) — each one a masterclass in timeless songwriting and uncompromising execution. On record and on stage, HITTEN deliver with the kind of conviction that made rock music matter in the first place.
